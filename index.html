About the Project

Uzbek World is a bilingual digital platform that helps people abroad learn the Uzbek language and explore Uzbekistan's cultural heritage.

The problem

Quality Uzbek learning materials in English are hard to find, and language is rarely taught together with the culture behind it.

Our solution

Step-by-step lessons, short quizzes and cultural stories in one place, in English and Uzbek.
